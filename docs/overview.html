Key — Reading the Data
Schedule Density

B2B (Back-to-Back) — Games played with zero days of rest: the team played the day before and plays again today.

3-in-4 — Every stretch of 3 consecutive games that spans 4 calendar days or less (measured from the first game to the last).

4-in-6 — Every stretch of 4 consecutive games spanning 6 calendar days or less.

5-in-8 — Every stretch of 5 consecutive games spanning 8 calendar days or less. These three stats overlap by design — a brutal 5-in-8 stretch will usually contain a couple of the shorter stretches too.

Rest Comparisons

Rest Vs — For every game, compare this team's days of rest against that same game's opponent: +1 if this team had more rest, −1 if fewer. Either club's season opener is excluded (no prior game to compare). Summed across the season — league-wide this always nets to exactly 0.

Waiting — Games where this team had at least one day off (not on a back-to-back itself) while that night's opponent was playing the second half of their back-to-back.

Tired — The mirror of Waiting: games where this team was on the second half of a back-to-back while the opponent had rest. League-wide, Waiting and Tired totals always match exactly.

Avg Rest Days — Average days between games. Excludes any gap that fully spans the Christmas break (Dec 23–25, 2026) or the All-Star break (Feb 4–7, 2027) — including whatever extra buffer days a specific team has attached to those breaks — so a league-mandated pause doesn't inflate a team's number.

Travel

Miles — Approximate season travel mileage: the straight-line (great-circle) distance between each consecutive game location on the schedule (own city when home, opponent's city when away). The two Global Series games route through the actual host city (Helsinki, Düsseldorf) rather than the "designated home" team's city. This is straight-line distance, not actual flight-path routing.

TZ Hours — Cumulative time-zone hours crossed across the season. For each consecutive pair of games, the shift is (previous game's hours-behind-Eastern) minus (this game's) — e.g. Pacific→Eastern is +3. The magnitude of every shift is summed for the season.

Schedule Shape

Longest Road Trip — The most consecutive away games in a single stretch this season.

Longest Home Stand — The most consecutive home games in a single stretch this season.

Toughest Stretch

The single worst 5-game window this season for the selected team. Every 5-game window is scored on road games, back-to-backs, TZ hours, and miles within that window, with each component normalized against that team's own other windows (so miles don't automatically dominate the score just because they're a bigger number). The highest-scoring window is surfaced as the callout on Team Explorer.

5-Game Segments

The season split into fixed, non-overlapping blocks of 5 games (the final block is 4, since 84 doesn't divide evenly) — the way a coaching staff actually talks about upcoming stretches, as opposed to Toughest Stretch's sliding window. The strip is shaded by this team's own range (which of MY segments is worst); the table is shaded against the league average for that same segment number (is this stretch tough because of us, or because that point in the season is tough for everyone). Click a row to expand the individual games in that segment.

Segment Tags

Simple threshold labels applied per segment — All Road / Homestand / Road Heavy (4 of 5 games away), B2B Heavy (2+ back-to-backs), Time Zone Grind (8+ TZ hours), Catching Tired Teams / Grinding Through Fatigue (based on Waiting vs. Tired counts within the segment), or Balanced if nothing crosses a threshold. A segment can carry more than one tag.

Reading the Tables

#5 top ~19% of the league (6 of 32) — favorable  ·  #28 bottom ~19% — unfavorable  ·  #16 everyone in between — neutral

Cell shading (Overview & tile ranks) — Each shaded cell's color is relative to that column's actual league average, not a fixed target — blue means better than average, red means worse, and how saturated the color is reflects how far from average that team sits.
