Key — Reading the Data

Definitions and conventions used throughout Schedule Lab.

Schedule Density

B2B (Back-to-Back) — Games played with zero days of rest: the team played the day before and plays again today.

3-in-4 — Every stretch of 3 consecutive games spanning 4 calendar days or less (measured from the first game to the last).

4-in-6 — Every stretch of 4 consecutive games spanning 6 calendar days or less.

5-in-8 — Every stretch of 5 consecutive games spanning 8 calendar days or less. These three stats overlap by design — a brutal 5-in-8 stretch will usually contain a couple of the shorter stretches too.

Rest Comparisons

Rest Vs — For every game, compare this team's days of rest against the opponent's: +1 if this team had more rest, −1 if fewer, 0 if equal. Either club's season opener is excluded (no prior game to compare). Summed across the season — league-wide this always nets to exactly 0.

Waiting — Games where this team had at least one day off while the opponent was on the second half of their back-to-back.

Tired — The mirror of Waiting: games where this team was on the second half of a back-to-back while the opponent had rest. League-wide, Waiting and Tired totals always match exactly.

Avg Rest Days — Average days between games. Excludes any gap spanning the Christmas break (Dec 23–25, 2026) or the All-Star break (Feb 4–7, 2027) so a league-mandated pause doesn't inflate a team's number.

Opponent Rest & Density

The same rest and density metrics computed for the opponent entering each matchup — not this team's own schedule, but what the other team was dealing with. Shown as split cards on the Schedule page and as columns in the main schedule table.

Opp B2B — The opponent was on a back-to-back entering this game.

Opp Rest — The opponent's actual days off before this game.

Rest +/− — Our rest days minus theirs. Positive = we were more rested; negative = they were. This is the per-game version of the season-level Rest Vs stat.

On the Full Schedule table, the Adv column shows ✓ when we had more rest, ✗ when the opponent did, and — when even or for openers.

Travel

Miles — Approximate season travel mileage: straight-line (great-circle) distance between each consecutive game location (own city when home, opponent's city when away). The Global Series games route through the actual host city (Helsinki, Düsseldorf) rather than either team's home city.

TZ Hours — Cumulative time-zone hours crossed. For each consecutive pair of games, the shift is (previous game's hours-behind-Eastern) minus (this game's) — e.g. Pacific→Eastern is +3. The magnitude of every shift is summed across the season.

Schedule Shape

Longest Road Trip — The most consecutive away games in a single stretch this season.

Longest Home Stand — The most consecutive home games in a single stretch this season.

Manual-Entry Columns (Schedule)

Some columns can't be derived from the NHL API and are entered by hand via the Game Editor. They appear as split cards and table columns on the Schedule page.

EA (Early Arrival) — Away games where the team arrived in the city more than one day before the game. Away only; shown as — on home games.

Hotel 25+ — Away games where the team's hotel was more than 25 minutes from the arena. Away only.

MDO (Mandatory Day Off) — A league-mandated day off before this game.

MS / DBS — Whether the team held a morning skate on game day (MS) or skated the day before (DBS).

ST (Special Teams) — Whether the team won or tied on special teams in this game.

11F / 7D — Whether the team dressed 11 forwards and 7 defencemen.

5-Game Segments (Team Explorer)

The season split into fixed, non-overlapping blocks of 5 games (the final block is 4, since 84 doesn't divide evenly). The strip is shaded by this team's own range across their segments — darker means a tougher block relative to their own season. The table cells are shaded against the league average for that same segment number — is this stretch tough because of us, or because that point in the season is tough for everyone? Click any row to expand the individual games in that segment, showing the full schedule detail including opponent rest and rest advantage.

Season Calendar (Team Explorer)

One cell per calendar day across the full season. Colors show the type of day at a glance:

Home game Away game Back-to-back Off day

Hover any game day to see the game number, date, opponent, and rest days.

Monthly Workload (Team Explorer)

Orange bars show how many games this team plays in each month. The number is printed above each bar and also appears in the hover tooltip.

The short blue tick inside each bar shows the NHL-wide average for that specific month — since the league doesn't spread games evenly across the season, each month has its own average rather than a single season-wide line.

Opponent Frequency (Team Explorer)

Total / Home / Away games against each of the other 31 teams this season. Click any column header to sort. Click a team row to expand the individual games against that opponent, showing the date and whether it was home or away.

Reading the Tables

#5 top ~19% of the league (6 of 32) — favorable  ·  #28 bottom ~19% — unfavorable  ·  #16 everyone in between — neutral

Cell shading — Each shaded cell's color is relative to that column's actual league average, not a fixed target. Blue means better than average, red means worse, and saturation reflects how far from average that team sits.

Rank chips appear on the Season at a Glance tiles in Team Explorer, showing where this team ranks among all 32 for that metric along with the league average.
